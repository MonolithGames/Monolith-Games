# Monolith Interactive Corp. Global Privacy Policy (GDPR & CCPA Compliant)

**Effective Date**: September 2026  
**Publisher**: Monolith Interactive Corp.  
**Contact**: legal@monolithgames.com  

## 1. Information Collection & Usage
Monolith Interactive Corp. ("Monolith") collects limited device telemetry, advertising identifiers (GAID), and in-app purchase receipts to provide mobile gaming, enterprise simulation, and platform services.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising & Consent (AdMob & UMP)
Our applications integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to respect user consent choices under GDPR, EEA, and CCPA regulations. Personalized advertising choices can be managed directly in the app Settings menu.

## 3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)
Transactions are processed securely by Google Play. Monolith does not store or access full payment card details.

## 4. Data Deletion Rights
Users may request complete deletion of telemetry data by contacting legal@monolithgames.com.
