# Monolith Calculator Privacy Policy

Monolith Calculator performs calculations and stores calculation history locally on your device. It does not require an account.

The app uses third-party services for advertising, privacy choices, and optional in-app purchases. These providers may process device identifiers, advertising information, purchase information, diagnostics, and app-interaction data according to their respective policies.

Google User Messaging Platform is used to present privacy choices where required. Privacy choices may be reviewed from the app's Settings screen.

Purchasing Remove Ads is processed through Google Play. Monolith Games does not directly receive or store payment-card information.

Calculation history can be deleted within the app or removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Contact: support@monolithgames.com  
Effective date: September 2026
