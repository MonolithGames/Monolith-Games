Monolith Calculator Monetization Strategy (Product 001)

1. AdMob Adaptive Banner Ad:
   - Positioned at the bottom of the screen away from calculation keys.
   - Non-intrusive, auto-adjusts to phone/tablet screen width.

2. One-Time In-App Purchase ($0.99 SKU: remove_ads):
   - Non-consumable product processed through Google Play Billing.
   - Immediately hides all ad banners across the application upon purchase or restore.

3. Privacy & Compliance:
   - Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK handles GDPR/EEA and regional privacy consent prior to loading ad units.
