# Monolith Calculator - Google Play Store Listing

## Title
Monolith Calculator

## Short Description
A clear, fast calculator with history, themes, and an ad-free option.

## Full Description
Monolith Calculator provides the essential calculation tools you need in a clean, approachable interface.

### Calculate Quickly
Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, percentages, decimals, and negative-number calculations.

### Review Your Work
View locally stored calculation history and copy results when you need them elsewhere.

### Make It Comfortable
Choose a light, dark, or system-matched theme. Large controls and clear labels make everyday calculations easy.

### Works Offline
Calculations and history work without an internet connection. Internet access is used to display advertisements and process optional purchases.

### Optional Ad-Free Upgrade
Purchase Remove Ads once through Google Play to hide advertising. Your purchase can be restored on supported devices using the same Google Account.

Monolith Calculator does not require an account. Calculation history remains stored locally on your device.
