# Monolith Agriculture & Farming Simulators

**Founder & Lead Architect**: Mike  
**Studio**: Monolith Games Inc.  

## 🌾 Overview
The `games/farm/` domain houses high-fidelity agricultural simulations, equipment databases, livestock logistics tools, and processing plant management games.

## 📂 Subdirectories & Catalogs
- **`FarmMonolith/`**: Kotlin / Compose Native Farm Simulator Engine.
- **`Compact & Utility Tractors Catalog/`**: Utility machinery database.
- **`Harvester Attachments & Headers Catalog/`**: Specialized cutting header specifications.
- **`Slurry & Manure Handling Catalog/`**: Organic waste and soil nutrient management.
- **`Greenhouse & Hydroponics Supplies Catalog/`**: Closed-atmosphere crop cultivation.
- **`Dairy & Cheese Production Catalog/`**: Milk processing and cheese aging ledger.
- **`Grain Mill & Bakery Supply Catalog/`**: Flour milling and commercial bakery systems.
- **`Cotton & Textile Manufacturing Catalog/`**: Baling, spinning, and fabric tailoring.
- **`Commercial Root Crop Logistics Catalog/`**: Potato and sugar beet processing.
- **`Heavy Forage & Silage Management Catalog/`**: Fermentation and livestock feed systems.
